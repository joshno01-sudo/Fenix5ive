WHAT THIS PROGRAM DOES

Printer Supply Monitor watches the ink and toner in every printer on the
network -- the Latex 360, the LaserJet, and any other office printer -- and
tells you before one runs out mid-job.

It reads the levels straight from the printers themselves, so there is nothing
to install on them and nothing to keep up to date by hand. It also keeps a
count of the spare cartridges on the shelf, with a plus and minus button per
item, so you can see at a glance what needs ordering.

When something drops below a level you set, a window pops up on this computer
and an email goes out to whoever you choose.


HOW YOU WILL USE IT

  1. Start the program and press "Find printers on the network".
  2. Tick the printers you want watched and press Add.
  3. Set the alert levels and the email address in Settings.
  4. Leave it running. It checks every few minutes on its own.


TWO THINGS WORTH KNOWING

The printers need SNMP switched on -- it usually already is. You will find it
in each printer's own web page, under Networking. If a printer does not turn
up when you scan for it, that is the first thing to check.

Nothing is ever sent to the printers except a read request. The program cannot
change a printer's settings, cancel a job, or print anything.


Press Next to install.
