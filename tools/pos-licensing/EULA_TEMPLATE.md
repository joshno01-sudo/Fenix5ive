more machines than the purchased number of Seats, requires the purchase of
additional licenses.

(f) use the Software to violate any applicable law.

**periodically verifies the license over the internet** against Licensor's

anonymized (hashed) machine identifier, the machine's network name, and the
version of this Agreement accepted. No Customer Data is transmitted for
license verification. Customer consents to this verification.

If verification is impossible (e.g. internet outage), the Software
continues to operate for a limited offline period of approximately
[72 hours], after which its functions are suspended until verification
succeeds.

**Customer agrees that Licensor may remotely deactivate the License Key**
(i) upon termination of this Agreement, (ii) upon Customer's material
breach, including non-payment or use exceeding the licensed Seats or
Location, or (iii) if the License Key is compromised or used in violation
of Section 3. Deactivation disables the Software's operation but does not
delete Customer Data, which remains available for export as described in
Section 7.
